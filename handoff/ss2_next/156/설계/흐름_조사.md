
## summary
I mapped today's ranked flow (code read plus host-harness runs on the w152-int natives, Korean bundled ROM, run-ahead 2 with framegen, no adb or emulator) and designed a new one. I also prototyped and tested its native half in scratch.

Today, every «다음 상대» goes through: a reset (0.8 s), a 4–15 s waiting window (reel flips every 250 ms), a «상대 발견!» card with a 3-2-1 auto-go, the game's own character / style / card screens, and an unskipped story. In ranked the story and dialogue are not auto-skipped, so the player mashes A or waits about 43 s. Then comes the round call. That is 20–35 s per match, with a full manual pick every time.

**What «뚜뚜뚜뚜» is (INFERRED).** The app plays no sound of its own here. It is the waiting window: the reel flipping 4 times a second like a phone ringing, plus the 3-2-1 countdown, plus the game music.

**Possible cause of complaint (2) (INFERRED).** In 152 the app writes «결과를 기록했어요 · B 결과 바로 보기» at the KO frame. In portrait it goes on the banner; in landscape it is a toast. The player sees a "result" message the moment the match is decided, before the quote.

**New flow:**
- **One-time pick.** First entry goes to a native H_SETUP. The player uses the game's own select (character, colour/2P colour via look, style, cards) once. The app captures it and stores it as `pref.loadout`.
- **Every match after that.** A short fake search (1.5–2.5 s; 1.0–1.3 s for a held opponent), then a full-cover Java VS screen for 2.2 s. Under the cover, native replays the loadout:
  - cursor to my cell + A, with 2P colour via look_set_pick
  - style cursor written + A
  - card screen A → R(しない) → A, then my saved cards written straight to 0x57DC/DD
  - story and pre-fight dialogue skipped with A every 12 frames
  - all of this at 4× speed, muted
- **Hand-over.** Native freezes the core on the first frame of the round call, which is black. The VS fades in 0.22 s, then the game's own round call (3.5 s) plays and the fight starts. From button press to control is about 7.5–8.5 s, and the player never sees a select screen again.

**Prototype result (scratch league.c/look.c, about 180 added lines).** It passes my end-to-end harness test (30/30). It leaves t_ranked (30/30) and t_hub_look (28/28) identical to the 152 natives.

## design
## 0. User-facing flow (new)

### First entry (no `pref.loadout`)
1. Any entry point → league session → logo skip (46 frames) → native H_SETUP. This happens on the title row-3 A, or automatically on EXTRA_HUB_GO when the app has told native «no loadout».
2. The game's own character select, unchanged: header «캐릭터 선택», look colour (OPTION) and B 2P colour. All cards are unlocked in RAM during SCR 2/4 (league-session save folder).
3. Banner text: «랭크에서 쓸 내 캐릭터 — 한 번만 골라요 · A 결정 · B 2P색 · OPTION 컬러» (style screen: «← 수라 · → 나찰 · A»; card screen: «고른 카드는 다음 판부터 그대로»). A 3 s top chip says the same. 〔177 정리: 175부터 «B 2P색» 안내 없음 — B 도 보통 색 결정(166-4)〕
4. When the game leaves the card screen (first MODE 241 frame), native captures and freezes the core on that black frame. Captured: cell (0–13, 15 = random), style (0x574C), b2p (look_status v[6]), and card k values from 0x57DC/DD.
5. Java saves `pref.loadout`, then immediately goes into the «다음 상대» path below. The VS left panel slides in with a 0.6 s «저장했어요» chip.

### Every match (loadout known)
1. «다음 상대» (result card), or re-entry: the VS curtain covers the game box synchronously, before hubReturn, so the reset logo never shows.
2. **Search (1.5–2.5 s).** My panel is already filled. The opponent slot is a dimmed reel flipping every 120 ms; the last two flips take 200 and 300 ms. Centre text «상대 찾는 중 · · ·». Held opponent: 1.0–1.3 s with «기다리던 상대».
3. **Reveal.** holdPending (148 rule: held the moment it is seen). The opponent panel slides in from the right over 200 ms (Decelerate). The «VS» stamp scales 1.8→1.0 over 180 ms (Overshoot 2.0). A white flash fades from alpha 0.55 to 0 over 160 ms. One KEYBOARD_TAP haptic replaces today's LONG_PRESS.
4. **VS (2.2 s minimum; 2.8 s on the first VS of a session so «▼ 캐릭터 바꾸기» is noticed).** A top progress bar drains.
   - A or tap = start now (ignored for the first 0.4 s after reveal).
   - B or Back = leave (opponent stays held).
   - ▼ or OPTION = change character.
5. **Meanwhile, under the curtain:** native resets, navigates the title, replays the loadout at 4× with audio muted, and holds on the black round-call frame.
6. **Lift.** When native is ready and the minimum has passed: create the match record, call nativeHubVsGo, fade the VS over 220 ms. The stage fades in, then «자아 당당히 · 1회전» (3.5 s), then the fight.

**Timing.** Press → round call ≈ 3.7–4.7 s; press → control ≈ 7.5–8.5 s. Today: 20–35 s. Native needs 397–529 game frames ≈ 1.7–2.2 s at 4×. Even at 1× (no ff) it only adds about 2–3 s of waiting on the VS.

### Rematch (−4)
- rankRematch, as today (EmuActivity.java:2623-2633), plus the VS shown already revealed (tag «재대결 2/3», no search). Same replay, hold and lift.

### Changing character
- Entry points:
  - the VS ▼ (→ hubReturn(−6), opponent stays held, so after the setup the same opponent returns within 1.0–1.3 s)
  - a new small «캐릭터 바꾸기» pill on the result card (→ hubReturn(−6))
  - LeagueActivity my-card row «내 캐릭터 · 샬럿 나찰 · 쪽빛 · 카드 2장 [바꾸기 ›]» (starts the league with EXTRA_SETUP)
  - the title banner «A 랭크 매치 · 결과 카드에서 캐릭터 바꾸기»
- If the player backs out of the setup (char select → title), Java re-pushes the old loadout, so cancelling keeps the old character.
