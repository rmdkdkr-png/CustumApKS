
## summary
I built a native auto-nav prototype and tested it on the Linux harness against the real core. Once the player's pick is remembered (character, style, 2P colour, cards; colour comes from look's per-character memory), the ranked hub now goes from «다음 상대»/go to the first playable round with no player input.

Tests:
- 144 sweep runs, all OK: every character in both styles plus the random centre cell, with varied card pairs, 2P colour, opponents (including mirror matches) and stages. They ran on the bundled Korean ROM and the original JUE ROM, each in app mode (run-ahead 2 + frame generation) and plain, plus 6 runs each on the H1 and Korean 1.01 ROMs.
- Feature checks: 128/128 PASS.
- Regression (t_quote 15 cases × 2 ROMs, the native.c harness t_nh, t_ranked, t_hub, t_dual, t_practice): identical to the unmodified 152 build. The two numeric differences seen in t_practice were wall-clock jitter; base itself moved on a rerun, and that rerun matched the prototype exactly.

Measured, counting from the «다음 상대» reset (frames at about 60/s):
- Char select at +27 (today's -4 path takes +61).
- Fight scene, where Java drops the VS screen: +241…387, median 286 (about 4.8 s). The random cell reaches it at +441…625.
- First round: +458…604, median 503 (about 8.4 s).
- Frame counts are identical with run-ahead/frame generation on and off.

Clean signal for Java: hub status v[15] == 6. That is the fight scene (MODE 240, SCR 8/10/12 depending on the matchup). Both fighters are loaded on its first frame and the picture is up 12 frames later.

The «뚜뚜뚜뚜» is not native time. Native reset to char select is under 1 s. The wait is the Java search window (rankPick.waitMs, 4–9 s, up to 15 s) plus the 3 s countdown on the found card (INFERRED; the reel plays no audio).

A «tail» mode makes even match 1 work: the player picks once by hand, the pick is recorded at story start (so the VS screen can show both picks), and story and pre-fight line are skipped automatically. If anything stalls, auto gives up (phase 8) and hands input back; the manual pick is then recorded. An optional mute silences the game's select and story audio behind the VS screen.

NATIVE STATE (league.c:388-510), kept outside H so .prac stays 628 bytes (_Static_assert untouched)
- AP holds the remembered pick: on, cell (0-13 or 15 = random), sty, b2p, k1, k2, skip_taunt, tail, mute. Progress: ph, why, timers.
- MY holds the last manual pick.
- auto_start() runs from hub_go_fixed (league.c:512-519), i.e. after search+go and on the -4 «next/rematch» path.
- auto_tick (league.c:425-482) runs every real frame in H_PICK_ME, kind 0 only (league.c:728-742).
- While active it blocks all game input and injects through league_in_or; nav 4 uses the existing override. look gets the new ctx LOOK_CTX_AUTO (look.c:178, look.h:16): it owns colour/preview but ignores input.

Phases: v[15] value, what it does, and frames from the -4 reset (sweep):
- 1 BOOT: menu A (nav 1). On -4 with auto on, nav 2 presses A at d=8 and skips the two menu «down» presses (league.c:556-559). Char select at +27.
- 2 CHAR: waits for 0x572A = 04 and char-select frames ≥ 10, then nav_to(cell, A): write the neighbour cell into 0x5748/0x5746, tap toward the target, verify, press A. If still 04 after 24 frames, retry; after 3 retries, fail. look_set_pick(b2p) is called here, because the title menu clears it. Char confirmed (PH 8) at +91…113.
- 3 STYLE: while 0x572A = 04 on SCR 4/PH 8, write 0x574C and press A every 16 frames. Then the character's animation runs. Style done (PH 10) at +167…313. Timeout 900 frames, because the random roulette reaches ~386.
- 4 CARD: on SCR 6 + PH 10 only, after the question (06) has been seen. Press A to open the menu; on 0C press R until 0x573A = 1; press A («안받음»). The centre cell or a save with no cards goes straight to the story.
- 5 STORY: write 0x57DC/DD = (cell<<4)|(sty<<3)|k each frame, or FF. Press A every 8 frames on MODE 241 SCR 0 FIGHT 0. Story starts at +201…349.
- 6 SCENE: MODE 240, SCR ≥ 8. Java drops the VS screen here (picture up at +12 frames). With flag 1, A is pressed only while FIGHT = 1. Scene at +241…387 (median 286); random cell +441…625.
- 7 ROUND: L.fight_seen, then H_MATCH. Round at +458…604 (median 503).
- 8 FAIL: input returns to the user; the reason is in v[19].

Phase timeouts are 600/900/900/400/2400/1500 frames. All timers are hub frames, which stop while the game is paused.

From go at the title instead (-3 path), the fight scene comes at +268…372.
