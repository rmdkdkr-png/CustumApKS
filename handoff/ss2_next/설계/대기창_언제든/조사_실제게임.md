# Ranked wait screen and "league anytime": what real games do, and what fits Legacito

## Bottom line

- **The wait is short and fake, so don't make it longer to feel real.** In `RankCore.pick()` the search takes 2–6 s, plus more for MMR above 1450 (up to 12 s). A held opponent takes 1.2–2.4 s. The question is what fills those seconds, not how to survive minutes.
- **Two kinds of wait screen exist in the field:**
  - A short informative screen with a clock, an estimate, the search widening, your rank and progress, and tips (Hearthstone, Rocket League, Clash Royale, Chess.com).
  - Playing something while the queue runs, with the match "calling" you when found (SF6, GG Strive, Smash, Overwatch, Valorant, Rocket League).
  
  Both can be built here. The second is cheapest as an arcade-style "challenger" interrupt inside the league session.
- **"League anytime" should be read all three ways:**
  - (a) Shorter entry paths.
  - (b) A lockout always comes with a way out on the same screen.
  - (c) A soft "new season" and an archived "start over".
  
  Real games never lock players out for good. Penalties expire, a match abandoned before round 1 is void, and season resets keep a trophy of the previous peak.

## 1. What real games do during the wait

### Street Fighter 6
- **Training while queued.** Matchmaking can run while you are in Training (and Arcade or Combo Trials) inside Fighting Ground, but not from the Battle Hub. The search pauses whenever a menu is open, and leaving Fighting Ground cancels it. Source: player reports, not Capcom docs ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1364780/discussions/0/4340986888820219180/), [Lite review](https://litetheironman.medium.com/lite-reviews-street-fighter-6-bea6289d4504)).
- **Status bar.** A bar at the bottom of the screen shows "matchmaking" or "paused". Players say restarting a stuck search fixes it ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1364780/discussions/0/6513974776603313710)).
- **Search range.** Players choose Limited, Expanded or Worldwide. The game "will expand if it takes too long" ([EventHubs](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2024/mar/02/sf6-expanded-matchmaking-worldwide)).
- **Battle Hub cabinets.** You sit at a cabinet and wait for someone to sit opposite. In the beta you could train at a cabinet and receive challenges, which you could accept or decline ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/street-fighter-6/how-to-access-training-mode-in-street-fighter-6-beta), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/how-to/street-fighter-6-battle-hub-explained)).
- **"Here Comes a New Challenger".** This arcade screen still appears "during lab sessions or in the lobby", and players can customise it ([Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=346789)).
- **Game Center.** The Battle Hub has a rotating set of retro Capcom arcade games ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/all-retro-capcom-games-available-in-street-fighter-6-game-center-listed)).
- **A warning that applies to us.** A 2023 player report says the game froze when a match was found during training ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1364780/discussions/0/3815158655054109983/?ctp=3)). The handoff between "what you were doing" and "the match" is the fragile part.

### Tekken 8
- **Rank range set before queuing.** Players pick a range around their rank (±2 to ±6 at launch, later ±1, ±2 or ±3) ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/tekken8-ranking-guide), [patch 2.00.01](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/pt_br/news/tekken-8-patch-notes-v2-00)).
- **Practice while queued.** Players say they practise while queued and "the game knows to interrupt you every time" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1778820/discussions/0/4202490424036486343/)). One reviewer still asked for an idle training dummy during the wait ([fromthedeskofchaos](https://fromthedeskofchaos.substack.com/p/tekken-8-review)).
- **After a match.** The result screen has a rematch timer ([patch notes](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/es_mx/news/tekken-8-patch-notes-v2-02)).
- **Patch 3.00.** Winning a rematch after a loss gives a Revenge Bonus, and demotion only happens after two losses in a row ([3.00 notes](https://bandainamcoent.com/news/tekken-8-patch-notes-v3-00)).
- **Arcade Quest.** An offline mode that simulates touring arcades and ranking up against CPU avatars ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2023/10/tekken-8s-single-player-arcade-quest-looks-weird-and-wonderful-in-16-mins-of-gameplay), [realsport101](https://realsport101.com/article/tekken-8-arcade-quest-game-mode)). It is the closest commercial match to our simulated CPU ranked mode. Virtua Fighter 4's Quest mode did the same in 2002, with CPU opponents named after famous players who copy their tactics ([MobyGames](https://mobygames.com/game/10162/virtua-fighter-4-evolution)).

### Guilty Gear Strive
- **Standby.** Your avatar takes a Duel Station while you load into Training until someone challenges you. "Quick Start" goes from the main menu straight to standby ([EventHubs](https://eventhubs.com/news/2021/jun/13/guilty-gear-lobby-tips), [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2021/06/guilty-gear-strive-developers-backyard-volume-six)).
- **Ranked Match (version 1.48, August 2025).** No lobby, training available while searching, 10 placement matches per character ([patch notes](https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/news/post-2559/), [EventHubs](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2025/jul/25/guilty-gear-backyard-updates/)).
- **Complaint.** Players report sitting in training for over 5 minutes waiting ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1384160/discussions/0/4338734550481046475)).

### Other fighting games
- **KOF XV:** one review says you can practise while waiting, "like an arcade where you play the CPU while others queue". Only that one source ([gamingnexus](https://www.gamingnexus.com/Article/7529/The-King-of-fighters-XV/)).
- **Granblue Fantasy Versus Rising:**
  - You can queue and practise in Training ([TheSixthAxis](https://thesixthaxis.com/2024/01/03/granblue-fantasy-versus-rising-review)).
  - The version 1.50 update (August 2024) added minigames in online lobbies "while waiting for a match" ([mp1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/granblue-fantasy-versus-rising-update-1-51-adds-versusia-and-more)).
- **Mortal Kombat 1 (what not to do):** at launch there was no training while queued and no way to back out of ranked once queued. Players called it "a huge miss" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1971870/discussions/0/3935643263196977940)).
- **Smash Ultimate:**
  - The Online Practice Stage ("waiting room", music track "Practice Fights") lets you warm up while waiting ([SmashWiki](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Online_Practice_Stage)).
  - Background Matchmaking lets you play other modes. A progress tab sits in the top-right corner and the match "begins as soon as an opponent has been found" ([SmashWiki](https://ssbwiki.com/Background_Matchmaking)).

### Other competitive and mobile games
- **Overwatch "While You Wait" (2019):** pick Practice Range, Skirmish, Deathmatch or a custom game while queued. You don't leave the queue between them ([Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/114974/overwatch-143-ptr-patch-notes-changes-how-you-wait)).
- **Valorant patch 13.06 (22 Sep 2026):** you can enter the Range while queued, or start the queue from inside it, and you get a notification when a match is found ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/valorant/Patch_13.06)). Riot had held this back for years because of server cost ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/riot-pre-match-aim-trainer-valorant)).
- **Rocket League:**
  - Online Free Play can be started "while searching for a game" or from the post-match screen ([Epic](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-202300000001622/c-202300000001682/online-free-play-rocket-league-a202300000010866)).
  - An Average Wait Time counter shows in the search window and on the post-match screen, shown as "> 10 Minutes" past 10 minutes ([Epic](https://www.epicgames.com/help/rocket-league-c-202300000001622/gameplay-c-202300000001682/what-is-the-average-wait-time-counter-in-rocket-league-a202300000014527?lang=sv)).
  - The Quick Play button reruns your last search ([Epic](https://www.epicgames.com/help/en-US/rocket-league-c5719357623323/c7261959845275/rocket-league-a5720133218203?lang=en-US)).
- **Hearthstone:** the Finding Opponent screen shows random tips picked by game mode, with separate versions for touch screens. A mechanical spinner rocks back and forth and settles on a description such as "Worthy Opponent" ([Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Finding_Opponent), [Tips](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Tips)).
- **Search widening, as players see it:**
  - Clash Royale: ±1 King Level, widening by 1 every 5 s up to ±3 at 10 s ([Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/matchmaking-changes/)).
  - Pokémon UNITE: widens to distant servers after 90 s, previously 60 s ([Bulbagarden](https://bulbagarden.net/threads/pokemon-unite-to-update-to-v1-18-2-5-on-april-17th-bringing-more-adjustments-to-improve-matchmaking.306471/)).
  - Chess.com: ±25 rating, then ±50 ([support](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8639319-how-does-matchmaking-work-in-live-chess)).
- **I could not confirm** the details of the Brawl Stars, Clash Royale and Pokémon UNITE search screens (cancel button, estimate, player count).

### The psychology of waiting
- Maister's rules: busy time feels shorter than idle time, uncertain waits feel longer than known ones, and unexplained waits feel longer than explained ones ([Maister](https://davidmaister.com/articles/5/52/index.html)).
- Practitioners add: never let an estimate jump upward, and say on screen when the search widens ([Qminder](https://qminder.com/video-game-queue-lessons), [bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-matchmaking-queue-ui-not-showing-estimated-wait)).

## 2. Leaving matches, lockouts, quick re-queue

- **Leaving before round 1 voids the match.** In Valorant, a remake before the first round ends means no RR change and the match doesn't appear in history ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/valorant/how-to-remake-in-valorant/)).
- **Penalties grow but expire.** League of Legends queue-dodge penalties step up (6 min/−5 LP, then 30 min, then 12 h) and drop a tier after 24 h ([Riot](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/queue-dodging)). Smash suspensions grow with repeat offences and must be waited out ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.co.uk/Support/Nintendo-Switch/Troubleshooting/Temporarily-Suspended-from-Online-Play-Super-Smash-Bros-Ultimate--1938351.html)). Pokémon UNITE Fair Play points recover by up to 5 a day; guides give different thresholds for losing ranked ([progameguides](https://progameguides.com/pokemon/what-are-fair-play-points-in-pokemon-unite/)). None of these is permanent.
- **Disconnects.** SF6 added a screen in December 2024 telling you the opponent disconnected ([EventHubs](https://eventhubs.com/news/2024/dec/06/unlisted-disconnect-message-sf6-punishment)). A Smash disconnect still costs the leaver GSP ([SmashWiki](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Quickplay)).
- **Quick re-queue paths:**
  - Rocket League's Quick Play button.
  - GG Strive's Quick Start.
  - Overwatch's "While You Wait" keeps the queue between matches.
  - In SF6, players report the queue stays live after a ranked match.
  - Tekken 8's rematch timer.

## 3. Season resets

- **SF6:** MR (the top-league rating) reset every 3 months; LP has never been reset. From 2025, resets scale MR instead of returning everyone to 1500 ([EventHubs](https://eventhubs.com/news/2024/nov/01/capcom-updates-sf6-ranked-mr), [KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/street-fighter-6-ranked-will-have-no-lp-reset/)).
- **Tekken 8 Season 3 (March 2026):** main characters drop up to 4 ranks, never below 6th Dan. Two title plates mark the Season 2 peak. Ranks are now per character ([3.00 notes](https://bandainamcoent.com/news/tekken-8-patch-notes-v3-00)).
- **Brawl Stars:** monthly soft reset, with a "Rank Boost" based on last season's peak ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/brawl-stars-ranked-guide-july-2026)).
- **The common pattern:** a soft reset that keeps identity, shows the old peak, and makes climbing back faster. Hard wipes are rare.

"League anytime": the three readings

- **(a) Entry from anywhere.**
  - Add a launcher card or Android app shortcut «랭크 매치» that opens the league session straight into a search (`EXTRA_HUB_GO` already exists). This is the Quick Play / Quick Start idea.
  - Add «리그 대전으로» to the in-game pad bar, so it isn't reachable only from title row 3. The normal session autosaves, so coming back is cheap.
  - Keep [다음 상대] on the result card as the fast re-queue (46 frames today).
- **(b) Never stuck.** Every block screen should carry its own fix, using in-view Ask cards only:
  - `leagueBlocked`: add «기권하고 새로 찾기» (`League.forfeit` then search), and for a match not yet started, «무효로 닫기».
  - Version or ROM mismatch on a started match: offer forfeit inline, not just "go to the league screen".
  - `dbProblem`: offer «기록 옆으로 치우고 새로 시작», plus the file path and a retry.
  - Keep "left before round 1 = void" (the Valorant precedent) and no penalty beyond that.
- **(c) Start over.** In the league screen, offer:
  - «새 시즌 시작» as a soft reset: LP pulled 50% toward the baseline (the rule already written in `ranked.md`), MMR kept, the old season archived with a peak title plate («시즌 1 최고 검호 ★2», after Tekken 8).
  - «처음부터», which renames `league.json` to an archive file (never deletes), picks a new seed, and goes back to the 5 placement matches.
  
  This screen is outside gameplay, so a dialog is safe there.

## 5. Most applicable patterns, in order
1. **Busy, honest search card (A + B).** Estimate that never jumps up, widening line, LP progress, today's record, game tips, avatar spinner. Low cost, high return.
2. **Every lockout has an inline exit (b).** Forfeit, void or new search from the same card. No permanent lockout, matching LoL, Smash and UNITE.
3. **One-tap entry and re-queue (a).** Launcher card or app shortcut «랭크 매치», a pad-bar entry, and keep [다음 상대] as the default.
4. **Soft "new season" plus archived "start over" (c).** Following Tekken 8 S3, Brawl Stars and SF6, with old records kept, never deleted.
5. **Optional "play arcade while searching, challenger at round break" (C).** The most "real" option, using existing reset paths. Do it after 1–4.
